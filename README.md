## 🌟 Project Story
Every call tells a story. A frustrated customer, a delayed service, or a happy resolution.

In this project, I analyzed thousands of real call center interactions to understand **why customers call, how agents respond, and where we can improve the customer experience.**

This is not just data analysis, this is about understanding human conversations behind the numbers.

## ❓ Business Problems We Solved
- Why is the call volume high on specific days?
- What are the most common reasons for customer calls?
- How long does it take to resolve a customer's issue?
- Which agents are performing well and who needs training?
- How is customer satisfaction (CSAT) changing over time?

## 📂 Dataset Information
The dataset includes:
- `Call ID, Customer ID, Call Duration, Wait Time`
- `Call Reason / Category (Billing, Technical, Inquiry, Complaint)`
- `Agent ID, Agent Performance`
- `Customer Satisfaction Score (1-5)`
- `Call Date, Call Time, Resolution Status`

## 🔍 My Analysis Process

### 1️⃣ Data Wrangling
Cleaned raw call logs, handled missing values, converted time formats, and created new features like Hour of Day, Day of Week.

### 2️⃣ Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Found hidden patterns in call timings, duration, and customer behavior.

### 3️⃣ Visualization & Insights
Created 15+ powerful charts to make data speak.

## 📊 Key Dashboards & Findings

**🔹 Call Volume Insights:**
- Monday & Friday are peak days with 40% more calls
- 10 AM - 2 PM is the busiest time slot
- Average call duration: 4.5 minutes

**🔹 Reason for Calls:**
- 35% Billing Issues
- 28% Technical Support
- 20% Product Inquiry
- 17% Complaints

**🔹 Agent Performance:**
- Top 5 agents have 95% resolution rate with 4.8+ CSAT
- Average handling time varies significantly between agents
- Training needed for agents with low satisfaction scores

**🔹 Customer Experience:**
- Calls waiting > 3 mins have 60% less satisfaction
- 85% of calls are resolved in first interaction
- Technical issues take longest to resolve

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Python:** Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn for deep analysis
- **Excel:** Initial cleaning and pivot analysis
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard (optional)
- **SQL:** For querying call logs

## 💡 Strategic Recommendations

1.  **Increase staffing** during Monday and 10AM-2PM slot to reduce wait time.
2.  **Create FAQ & Self-Service** for Billing Issues to reduce 35% of calls.
3.  **Agent Training Program** focused on Technical Issue handling.
4.  **Callback System** if wait time exceeds 2 minutes to improve CSAT.
5.  **Knowledge Base** update for agents to improve First Call Resolution.
